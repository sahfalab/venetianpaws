Our Philosophy

Rooted In Care
Refined By Experience

Venetian Paws was born from a simple belief: your companion deserves the same caliber of attention and devotion that defines island living on Belle Isle.

We don't do ordinary. Every walk follows scenic waterfront paths. Every overnight stay is an experience in comfort. Every detail is considered — because they would do the same for you.

Meet Sofia

Our resident Coton

Sofia is the heart and soul of Venetian Paws — a Coton de Tulear with an extraordinary gift for making every dog feel at ease. Whether it's a first-time visitor or a longtime guest, Sofia greets them all with the same warmth.

From there she reads the room — playing with those who want to play, keeping quiet company with those who'd rather just settle. She joins our walks, stays close to overnight guests,and has a quiet gift for making every dog feel at home.
